Frequently Asked Questions.

Find answers to the most common questions about our services.

Which website package should I choose?

Starter is a great foundation, Advanced offers more custom sections, and Premium is for a fully bespoke Shopify experience.

What is the difference between Advanced and Premium?

Advanced is built on Wuilt with expanded styling and sections. Premium is built on Shopify with a more deeply customized experience.

Can I add more products after delivery?

Yes. You can manage your products through your store dashboard, or contact us if you need help adding more.

Is the domain included?

Domain registration is handled separately and depends on the domain you choose.

Do you provide support after delivery?

Every package includes seven days of support after delivery.
